PART 3: OBJECTION SCRIPTS
OBJECTION: "It's too expensive" / "Can you do it cheaper?"


NEVER: drop the price. It signals that the price was wrong to begin with.


DO: offer a different product at a lower price point.


RESPONSE:


"I get it — we don't do discounts on the boat, but I can offer you a couple of options:


1. Half-day tour: same boat, same quality, 4 hours instead of 8. Covers Pakleni Islands and swimming. Starts at €[X].
2. A different route: the Pakleni route is shorter and less fuel, so we can price it slightly lower than Vis.


Which works better for your group?"


OBJECTION: "We found something cheaper elsewhere"


RESPONSE:


"Happy for you to compare — I'd just ask you to check what's included. A lot of boats in Hvar charge extra for fuel, drinks, and equipment. Ours is all-in: fuel, towels, drinks (water, beer, wine, soft drinks), snorkelling gear, captain.


If the other option is genuinely all-in and still cheaper, that's a fair deal. But if it's base price only, the gap is probably smaller than it looks."


OBJECTION: "Can you guarantee Blue Cave?"


RESPONSE:


"No one can guarantee it — Blue Cave is weather-dependent and run by the national park. If conditions don't allow on the day, we go to Green Cave, Stiniva, and the Blue Lagoon instead.


Stiniva is one of the most photographed beaches in Europe. The Blue Lagoon is stunning for swimming. Most guests who go on both days actually prefer the backup route.


If Blue Cave is a hard requirement, I'd suggest booking early morning when the park opens — best chance of entry."


OBJECTION: "We're not sure about the date yet"


RESPONSE:


"No problem. I'll keep the date tentatively open on my end — no pressure. When you have a date, just let me know and I'll check availability. Peak season books fast so the sooner the better, but I don't want you to rush."

"Absolutely — you can find us on Google and TripAdvisor as Mini Yachts Hvar. We also have guest photos on Instagram @miniyachtshvar. Happy to send you a few directly if you'd like."

PART 5: LEAD SCORING


HOT — reply within 30 minutes, full 3-email follow-up:
- Date within 2 weeks
- Group size clear, route clear, budget signals present
- Celebration mentioned
- High-effort, detailed inquiry (clearly wrote just to you)


WARM — reply within 60 minutes, full 3-email follow-up:
- Date 2-8 weeks out
- Some details missing but fixable
- Normal inquiry tone


WEAK — reply within 60 minutes, 1 follow-up only:
- No date or "sometime in summer"
- Price-only question with nothing else
- Copy-paste feel (no personalization, no specifics)
- Unrealistic request (Blue Cave + Vis + South Hvar in one 8-hour day)
