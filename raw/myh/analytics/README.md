# MYH Analytics Source Notes

## Data period
Most Wix exports cover 20 September 2025 to 20 September 2026.

The device export currently covers 21 August 2026 to 20 September 2026 only.

## Source
Wix Analytics exports.

## Files included
- Wix traffic report
- Wix page visits
- Wix traffic by device
- Wix Google Search queries
- Wix Google Search pages
- Wix top searches
- Wix conversions
- Wix conversion clicks
- Wix form actions
- Wix button clicks

## Interpretation rules
- These reports show tracked traffic and onsite actions, not confirmed boat bookings.
- A click, form action or conversion should be treated as an interest signal unless it can be matched to a confirmed inquiry or booking.
- Google Search reports inside Wix may differ from Google Search Console because the platforms use different data sources and reporting methods.
- The exact date range must be checked before comparing reports.
