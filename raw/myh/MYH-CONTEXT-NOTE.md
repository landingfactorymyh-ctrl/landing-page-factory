# Mini Yachts Hvar — Initial Context

MYH is the first live case study for the Landing Page Factory.

## Business
Premium private boat tours, charter experiences, transfers and related
premium tourism services based around Hvar.

## Initial audience
English-speaking leisure travelers, primarily US and UK guests:
couples, families and groups of friends.

## Current traffic sources
Google Business Profile / Google Maps and Google organic search.
Analytics audit pending.

## Primary conversion action
Email enquiry form.

## Typical sales flow
Guests usually enquire about availability for specific dates.
MYH replies with one to three suitable boat options, route/itinerary guidance,
inclusions, pricing and calm concierge-style support.

## Brand differentiator
Premium calm, warm authority, fast human responsiveness, local expertise,
transparent guidance and the feeling that the guest's day is handled.

## First research objective
Use Google Search Console, Wix Analytics, GBP performance data and inquiry
data to identify the highest-value, highest-intent page type and CTA before
building the first landing page.

## Possible page hypotheses
1. Private full-day boat experience page
2. Boat shortlist / date-and-group-size enquiry page
3. Hvar–Dubrovnik via Korčula private-transfer page

## Explicitly later
Packages, corporate groups, travel-agent/B2B pages, press/PR, overnight
multi-day charter pages, e-foil-focused acquisition and live agents.
